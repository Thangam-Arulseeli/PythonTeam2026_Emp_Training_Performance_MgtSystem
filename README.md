Project Description
===================
The Employee Training & Performance Management System is an application used to manage employee training programs, trainee details, attendance, assessments, performance evaluations and training reports.

The application should provide the following functionalities:
1. Employee/Trainee Management
2. Trainer Management
3. Training Course Management
4. Training Batch Management
5. Training Enrollment
6. Attendance Management
7. Assessment Management
8. Performance Evaluation
9. Training Reports and Analytics
10. Authentication and Authorization
